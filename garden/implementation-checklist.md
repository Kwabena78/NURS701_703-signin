# Chartwell front yard: implementation checklist

Follows `chartwell-front-yard-plan.md` (Section 7 timing). Quantities are **provisional**, taken from the plan's plant schedule, until you confirm measurements.

## Inputs still needed (blocks the final shopping list)

- [ ] Compass direction the front faces
- [ ] Frontage width (m) and kerb-to-house depth (m)
- [ ] Land covenant or subdivision guidelines (fence height, bins, planting rules)
- [ ] Hedge leaf ID (lilly pilly = myrtle rust host)

## Stage 0: Checks (this week, no spend)

- [ ] Phone the utility box owner (script in `phone-scripts.md`)
- [ ] Confirm front fence/screen height with Hamilton City Council (script)
- [ ] Read the covenant; note bin, fence and planting clauses
- [ ] Identify the hedge shrubs
- [ ] Request service plans at beforeudig.co.nz
- [ ] Dig a 400 mm test hole in bed A, fill with water, time the drain (over 24 h = drainage work needed)

## Stage 1: Hardscape and edging (October to November)

- [ ] Steel edging (black, ~100 mm) on all lawn and bed lines
- [ ] Remove grass strip between path and driveway (Zone C)
- [ ] Bin screen (only after council/covenant check)
- [ ] Lighting: licensed electrician for the transformer

## Stage 2: Planting (November)

- [ ] Enlarge bed A; loosen soil, add compost, mound 150 mm if drainage is poor
- [ ] Lomandra x12 (bed A) and x8 (strip C), PB5
- [ ] Astelia 'Silver Spear' x3, PB6.5
- [ ] Corokia 'Geenty's Green' x8 around the utility box, PB5 (clear the box door)
- [ ] Liriope or Dianella x18 in front of hedge D
- [ ] Two black pots with Corokia or Pittosporum spheres at the door
- [ ] Muehlenbeckia axillaris x10 along the foundation
- [ ] Basalt chip 10-14 mm over weed mat in strip C; mulch beds 50-75 mm
- [ ] Water twice a week through summer

## Stage 3: Autumn 2027

- [ ] Plant kōwhai (PB28 or larger, multi-stem); stake for 2 years
- [ ] Replace hedge with Griselinia 'Broadway Mint' x8 if lilly pilly (PB12-PB18)
- [ ] Feed and overseed the lawn

## Sourcing

Wairere Nursery (Gordonton Rd), Oderings, Palmers, Waikato native nurseries (Waikato Regional Council list). Get two quotes; check stock and current NPPA status before buying.
