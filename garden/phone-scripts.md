# Phone scripts

## 1. Utility box owner (label on the green box will name the company)

"Hi, I'm at 39 [street], Chartwell. There's a [fibre/gas/power] cabinet on my front lawn. I'm planting a low hedge nearby. Which sides must stay clear, and by how many centimetres? Are there any plants you don't allow? Can you confirm in writing or by email?"

Write down: name, date, reference number, clearance distances.

## 2. Hamilton City Council (planning duty planner)

"Hi, I'm at 39 [street], Chartwell, [zone]. I want to build a 1.2 m black batten screen beside the side fence to hide bins, forward of the front building line. What is the maximum height for a fence or screen there? Does it need building or resource consent? Does my subdivision covenant or consent notice add any limits?"

Write down: name, date, rule cited, whether consent is needed.

## 3. Covenant (email to the developer or your lawyer, if unclear)

"Please send the land covenant or design guidelines for 39 [street]. I need the clauses on front fences, bin screening, planting and approved plant lists."
